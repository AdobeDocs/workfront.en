# Coworker overview

Coworker Chat is a conversational interface for getting work done. You describe a goal in plain language, and Coworker plans the work, executes it across your Adobe and connected systems, validates the results, and brings the finished work back to you for approval.

Coworker is replacing the current AI Assistant as a more powerful way to get work done. 

Coworker is part of the Adobe ecosystem, and is not just limited to Workfront. 

For more information on Coworker and its capabilities, see [Adobe CX Enterprise Coworker Chat overview](https://experienceleague.adobe.com/en/docs/cx-enterprise-coworker/content/chat/overview).

For skills available in Coworker in Workfront, see [Coworker skills](/help/quicksilver/workfront-basics/coworker-in-workfront/coworker-skills.md).
