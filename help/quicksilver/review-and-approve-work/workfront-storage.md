# Move to Workfront on Adobe cloud storage

Workfront on Adobe cloud storage enables the full unified review and approval experience: reviews in the Frame.io viewer, powerful approval workflows, cross-product visibility of assets, and more.

This article is the starting point for Workfront administrators moving to Adobe cloud storage. It explains what happens when your organization is enabled, points you to the details of what's different, and links to the articles that help you plan and roll out the change.

>[!NOTE]
>
>You must be on a version of Workfront that supports Adobe cloud storage. If your organization isn't already on a supported version, contact your Adobe account representative.

## What happens when your organization is enabled

When your organization is enabled for Adobe cloud storage at renewal, nothing changes right away. Your existing projects keep the storage model and approval service they use today, and any new project you create stays on legacy Workfront storage until you decide otherwise.

The new experience turns on only when a Workfront administrator sets Adobe cloud storage as the default storage option in **Setup** > **System** > **Preferences**. From that point, new projects use Adobe cloud storage, which includes the Frame.io viewer and Unified Approvals. Existing projects don't change.

Because you control the timing, you can test the experience, update your Workfront Fusion scenarios, and plan for reporting changes before anyone works in it. For step-by-step instructions, see [Enable Adobe cloud storage for your organization](/help/quicksilver/administration-and-setup/set-up-workfront/configure-system-defaults/enable-esm.md).

## How approvals work on Adobe cloud storage

Adobe cloud storage projects use Unified Approvals by default, with review and approval in the Frame.io viewer. Objects that stay on legacy Workfront storage keep whatever approval service they already use—legacy document approvals or Unified Approvals.

>[!NOTE]
>
>Setting Adobe cloud storage as the default doesn't change the approval service already in use on your legacy Workfront storage objects. Unified Approvals is the default only for new objects created on Adobe cloud storage.

Before you roll out Adobe cloud storage, make sure the people who manage approvals know what to expect. For more information, see [Move from legacy document approvals to Unified Approvals](/help/quicksilver/review-and-approve-work/move-to-unified-approvals.md).
