# Review a grouped approval

{{highlighted-preview-article-level}}

When you're assigned to a grouped approval, all the assets in the group appear together so you can make a decision on each one. You can approve or request changes on every asset at once, or make a decision on each asset individually.

This article covers what's specific to reviewing a group of assets. For the mechanics of making a single decision, adding markup, and the other places you can approve from, see [Approve a document in Workfront](/help/quicksilver/review-and-approve-work/document-reviews-and-approvals/review-and-approve-documents/approve-a-document.md), [Review a document in Workfront](/help/quicksilver/review-and-approve-work/document-reviews-and-approvals/review-and-approve-documents/review-a-document.md), and [Review and approve with the Frame.io viewer](/help/quicksilver/review-and-approve-work/document-reviews-and-approvals/review-with-frame.md).

>[!IMPORTANT]
>
>The content of this article refers to updated document approval functionality that is only available for specific accounts. For information on standard approval processes, see the articles listed in [Work approvals](/help/quicksilver/review-and-approve-work/manage-approvals/manage-approvals.md).

## Review a grouped approval from the My Approvals widget in Home

The My Approvals widget in Home groups the assets under a single approval so you can act on all of them together.

>[!NOTE]
>
>You may have to add the My Approvals widget to your Home page. For more information, see [Add, edit, or remove widgets in Home](/help/quicksilver/workfront-basics/using-home/using-the-home-area/add-edit-remove-widgets-in-new-home.md).

1. Click the **Home** icon in the upper-left corner of Adobe Workfront, then find the **My Approvals** widget.

1. Open the grouped approval. All the assets in the group are listed together, with the number of items in the group at the top.

1. Make a decision using one of the following:

   * **Mark all approved** — records an approved decision on every asset in the group at once.
   * **Mark all needs work** — records a needs-work decision on every asset in the group at once.
   * **Make decision** — make an individual decision on a single asset. Repeat for each asset you want to decide on separately.

## Review a grouped approval in the Frame.io viewer

In the Frame.io viewer, all the assets in the group display together so you can make a quick decision on each one, or open an asset to review it in detail.

1. Go to your review email notification and click **Go to review**, or open the grouped approval from the **My Approvals** widget in Home.

1. Do one of the following:

   * Make a quick decision on each asset directly from the group page, without opening it.
   * Click an asset to open it, add comments or markup, then use the arrow to move to the next asset in the group. The asset counter (for example, **1 of 2**) shows your place in the group.

   For more information on commenting and markup in the Frame.io viewer, see [Review and approve with the Frame.io viewer](/help/quicksilver/review-and-approve-work/document-reviews-and-approvals/review-with-frame.md).

1. Choose a decision for each asset:

   * **Approved** — the asset does not need changes and is ready for use.
   * **Needs Work** — the asset needs changes and is not ready for use.

   For more information about decisions, see [Document decision status overview](/help/quicksilver/review-and-approve-work/document-reviews-and-approvals/manage-document-approvals/document-approval-status.md).
